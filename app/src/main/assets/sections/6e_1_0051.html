06.E  Harmful Plants, Animals and Insects

06.E.01–06.E.04

06.E.01  Protection against hazards from insects and/or animals harboring fleas or disease-carrying insects shall include, as applicable, the following:

  1. PPE such as netted hoods, leather work gloves, and high-top work boots worn in conjunction with trousers and long-sleeved shirts;

  2. Clothing treated at the factory with DEET or Permethrin are recommended in areas of high insect population;

  3. Drainage or spraying of breeding areas;

  4. Destroying or flagging (marking as hazard) of nests;

  5. Smudge pots and aerosols for protecting workers and small areas;

  6. Elimination of actions or conditions that propagate insects or vermin;

  7. Extermination measures by a certified pesticide applicator or, for over the counter items, following the instructions on the label;

  8. Approved first aid procedures for employees; employees allergic to bee stings shall be encouraged to self identify to the supervisor and to carry an EpiPen;

  9. Inoculation against diseases known to be a local hazard; and

  10. Instruction in recognition of the animals and insects and their common nesting habits, aggressiveness, etc.

06.E.02  In areas where there is exposure to poisonous snakes or lizards, employees shall be required to:

  1. Wear snake chaps or knee-high snake boots worn in conjunction with trousers and long-sleeved shirts;

  2. Be trained in recognition of the snakes and their common nesting habits, aggressiveness, etc.; and

  3. Be trained in the proper first aid procedures for bites.

06.E.03  In areas where employees are exposed to poisonous plants, the following protective measures, as applicable, shall be provided:

  1. Removal or destruction of plants, where practical;

  2. Appropriate protective clothing such as gloves;

  3. Protective ointments;

  4. Soap and water for washing exposed parts; and

  5. Instruction in recognition and identification of the plants.

06.E.04  When burning poisonous plants, controls shall be instituted to prevent contact with or inhalation of toxic elements contained in the smoke.
