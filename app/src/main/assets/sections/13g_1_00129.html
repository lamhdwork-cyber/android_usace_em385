13.G  Abrasive Blasting Equipment

13.G.01–13.G.04

13.G.01  Hose and hose connections shall be designed to prevent build up of static electricity.

13.G.02  Connections and nozzles shall be designed to prevent accidental disengagement. All connections shall be equipped with safety lashings. > See Sections 20.A.16 and 20.A.17.

13.G.03  Nozzle attachments shall be of metal and fit on the outside of the hose. A deadman-type control device shall be provided at the nozzle to cut off the flow if the operator loses control of hose. A support shall be provided on which the nozzle may be mounted when it is not in use.

13.G.04  Additional requirements on abrasive blasting are in Sections 5 and 6.
