09.K  Fire Watch

09.K.01–09.K.03

09.K.01  When fire watch personnel or guards are provided, they shall make frequent rounds through buildings and storage areas when work is suspended.

09.K.02  Smoke detectors shall be installed and maintained where personnel are quartered.

09.K.03  In any instance where combustible materials have been exposed to fire hazards (i.e., welding operations, hot metals, open flame, etc.), a fire watch shall be assigned to remain at the location for at least one (1) hour after the exposure has ended.
