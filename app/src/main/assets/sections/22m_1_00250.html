22.M  Vehicle-Mounted Elevating and Rotating Work Platforms (Aerial Devices/Lifts)

22.M.01–22.M.05

22.M.01  Vehicle-mounted elevating and rotating work platforms (aerial lifts, to include articulating boom platforms/lifts (knuckle boom lifts), trailer-mounted boom lifts) shall be designed and constructed per ANSI/SIA A92.2.

22.M.02  Vehicle-mounted elevating and rotating work platforms shall be operated, inspected, tested, and maintained as specified in the operating manual for that piece of equipment.

  1. Vehicle-mounted elevating and rotating work platforms shall also comply with requirements in Sections 18.G and 21.

  2. Records of inspections conducted while the unit is at the work site shall be maintained at the work site.

  3. All aerial devices shall have manufacturer's operating manual readily available in or on the vehicle.

  4. If the unit is considered rated, and used as an insulating device, copies of the electrical insulating components and system tests conducted while the unit is at the work site shall be maintained at the work site.

  5. All required safety decals, labels and signs shall be in place and readable.

22.M.03  Only personnel trained in the use of the vehicle-mounted elevating and rotating work platform shall be authorized to operate it. Training shall consist of:

  1. Reading and understanding the manufacturer's operating manual and any associated rules and instructions, or training by a QP on the contents on these documents; and

  2. Reading and understanding all decals, warnings, and instructions on the vehicle-mounted elevating and rotating work platform.

22.M.04  Transporting.

  1. Aerial lift trucks, to include cherry pickers, shall not be moved when the boom is elevated in a working position with personnel in the basket except for equipment that is specifically designed for this type of operation. When manufacturers allow mobile operation, the worksite shall be inspected for:

    1. Untamped earth fills (soft ground);

    2. Ditches;

    3. Drop-offs and floor obstructions;

    4. Debris;

    5. Overhead obstructions and electrical conductors;

    6. Weather conditions, and

    7. The presence of unauthorized persons.

  2. Before moving an aerial lift, the boom(s) shall be inspected to see that it is properly cradled and outriggers are in stowed positions, except as provided in a, above.

  3. Aerial ladders shall be secured in the lower traveling position by the locking device on top of the truck cab and the manually operated device at the base of the ladder before the truck is moved for highway travel.

22.M.05  Operating practices. The manufacturer's instruction for control station operation must be followed (e.g. primary versus secondary; upper versus lower).

  1. Brakes shall be set and outriggers, when used, shall be positioned on pads or a solid surface.

  2. Wheel chocks shall be installed before using an aerial lift on an incline.

  3. Lift controls shall be tested each day prior to use to ensure safe working condition.

  4. Lift controls will be located below the guardrail height. When lift controls are not located below the guardrail height, an after-market guard will be installed.

  5. Boom and basket load limits specified by manufacturer shall not be exceeded.

  6. Articulating boom and extensible boom platforms, primarily designed as personnel carriers, shall have both platform (upper) and lower controls.

    1. Upper controls shall be in or beside the platform within easy reach of the operator.

    2. Lower controls shall provide for overriding the upper controls.

    3. Controls shall be plainly marked as to their function.

    4. Lower level controls shall not be operated unless permission has been obtained from the employee in the lift except in case of emergency. This practice shall be documented in the applicable AHA.

  7. Climbers (spikes) shall not be worn while performing work from an aerial lift.

  8. The insulated portion of an aerial lift shall not be altered in any manner that might reduce its insulating value.

  9. Occupants shall always stand firmly on the floor of the basket and shall not sit or climb on the edge of the basket or use planks, ladders, or other devices for a work position.
