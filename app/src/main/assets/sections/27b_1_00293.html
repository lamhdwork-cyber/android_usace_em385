27.B  Concrete

27.B.01–27.B.03

27.B.01  Equipment.

  1. Bulk storage bins, containers, or silos shall have conical or tapered bottoms with mechanical or pneumatic means of starting the flow of material.

  2. Concrete mixers equipped with 1 yd3 (0.8 m3) or larger loading skip shall be equipped with a mechanical device to clear the skip of material and shall have guardrails installed on each side of the skip.

  3. Handles on bull floats used where they may contact energized electrical conductors shall be constructed of nonconductive material or insulated with a nonconductive sheath whose electrical and mechanical characteristics provide equivalent protection.

  4. Powered and rotating concrete troweling machines that are manually guided shall be equipped with a control switch that will automatically shut off the power whenever the operator removes his/her hands from the equipment handles.

  5. Concrete pumping systems using discharge pipes shall be provided with pipe supports designed for 100% overload.

  6. Handles of concrete buggies shall not extend beyond the wheels on either side of the buggy.

  7. Concrete buckets equipped with hydraulic or pneumatically operated gates shall have positive safety latches or similar safety devices installed to prevent premature or accidental dumping. The buckets shall be designed to prevent material from accumulating on the top and sides of the bucket.

  8. Sections of tremies and similar concrete conveyances shall be secured with wire rope (or equivalent material) in addition to the regular couplings or connections.

27.B.02  Structural and reinforcing steel for walls, piers, columns, and similar vertical structures shall be supported and/or guyed to prevent overturning or collapse. Support systems for reinforcing steel that are independent of other form or shoring support systems shall be designed by a Registered Professional Engineer (RPE).

  1. Connections of equipment used in plumbing-up shall be secured.

  2. The turnbuckles shall be secured to prevent unwinding while under stress.

  3. Plumbing-up guys and related equipment shall be placed so that employees can get at the connection points.

  4. Plumbing-up guys shall be removed only under the supervision of a Competent Person (CP).

  5. Measures shall be taken to prevent unrolled wire mesh from recoiling.

27.B.03  Post-tensioning operations.

  1. No employee (except those essential to the post-tensioning operations) shall be permitted to be behind jacks or end anchorages during post-tensioning operations.

  2. Signs and barriers shall be erected to limit employee access to the post-tensioning area during tensioning operations.
