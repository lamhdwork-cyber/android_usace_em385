09.H  Firefighting Equipment

09.H.01–09.H.03

09.H.01  Firefighting equipment shall be provided and installed in accordance with applicable NFPA and OSHA regulations.

09.H.02  No fire protection equipment or device shall be made inoperative or used for other purposes, unless specifically approved by the GDA.

09.H.03  If fire hose connections are not compatible with local firefighting equipment, adapters shall be made available.
