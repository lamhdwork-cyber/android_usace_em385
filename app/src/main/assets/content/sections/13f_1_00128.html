13.F  Chain Saws

13.F.01–13.F.07

13.F.01  Chain saws shall have an automatic chain brake or kickback device.

13.F.02  The idle speed shall be adjusted so that the chain does not move when the engine is idling.

13.F.03  See Section 5 for proper PPE.

13.F.04  Chain saws will not be fueled while running, while hot, or near an open flame. Saws will not be started within 10 ft (3 m) of a fuel container.

13.F.05  The operator will hold the saw with both hands during all cutting operations.

13.F.06  A chain saw must never be used to cut above the operators' shoulder height.

13.F.07  See Section 31 for tree maintenance and removal requirements.
