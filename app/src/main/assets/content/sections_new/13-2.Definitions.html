13-2 Definitions.

   a.  Authorized Instructor.  One who has been trained, authorized, and provided an authorized instructor's card by the tool manufacturer or by an authorized representative of the tool manufacturer.  (13-2.a)

   b.  Explosive-Actuated Tool.  A tool that uses the expanding gases from a powder load to drive a fastener.  Also referred to as a powder-actuated tool.  (13-2.b)
