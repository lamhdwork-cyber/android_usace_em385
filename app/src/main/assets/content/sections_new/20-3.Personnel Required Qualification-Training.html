20-3 Personnel Required Qualification/Training.

   a.  Qualified Inspectors (QI).  QIs must be certified by an ASME accredited Authorization Inspection Agency or Qualified Inspection Organization.  The QI may not be employed by the manufacturer.  (20-3.a)

   b.  Qualified Mechanics.  Mechanics who adjust or change the settings of safety relief valves must be qualified according to the ASME BPVC.  (20-3.b)
