36-5. Inspection Requirements.

Inspection must be performed by the SSHO and include qualitative and/or quantitative assessments to ensure the following:

   a.  The support zone, CRZ and EZ are defined and effective.  (36-5.a)

   b.  Exposure controls to include PPE are effective.  (36-5.b)

   c.  Decontamination procedures are being implemented and effective.  (36-5.c)

   d.  Occupational exposure sampling and monitoring equipment is being used properly and monitoring results documented.  (36-5.d)

   e.  Emergency response services have been arranged and rehearsed with local emergency response service providers.  (36-5.e)
