17-3 Personnel Required Qualification/Training.

   a.  All employees engaged in demolition or renovation activities must be instructed in the Demolition Plan so that they can conduct their work activities in a safe manner (see para 17-7).  (17-3.a)

   b.  Load Handling Equipment (LHE) Operators.  LHE Operators must be trained and qualified according to chapter 16.  (17-3.b)

   c.  Lead inspectors conducting surveys must meet the training requirements according to paragraph 6-3.  (17-3.c)

   d.  Asbestos inspectors conducting surveys must meet the training requirements according to paragraph 6-3.  (17-3.d)
