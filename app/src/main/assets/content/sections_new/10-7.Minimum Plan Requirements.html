10-7 Minimum Plan Requirements.

A written Housekeeping Plan is required for all welding, cutting, brazing, or heating operations that involve toxic materials.  Develop the plan according to chapter 35 of this manual, to include the following:

   a.  Detailed description of the activity being performed, to include the frequency, type, and volume of hot work activities.  (10-7.a)

   b.  Description of anticipated hazards or concerns and the control measures that will be implemented to control to an acceptable level, to include routine maintenance and housekeeping procedures and schedule.  For example, frequency that all surfaces need to be regularly high efficiency particulate air vacuumed and wet wiped so that they are free of contamination.  (10-7.b)

   c.  Assignment of personnel and their roles and responsibilities.  Include necessary information/documentation to support qualification/training of such persons.  (See para 10-3)  (10-7.c)

   d.  Inspection and oversight methods to ensure adherence to the plan.  (10-7.d)
