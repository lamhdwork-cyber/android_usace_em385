32-4 Roles and Responsibilities.

   a.  A Competent Person (CP).  See paragraphs 1-2 or 2-2, as applicable. Must prepare a Construction Safety and Phasing Plan (CSPP) and Safety Plan Compliance Document (SPCD) according to paragraph 32-7.  (32-4.a)

   b.  Airfield Managers will review and accept the CSPP and SPCD no later than

14 days prior to beginning work.  (32-4.b)
