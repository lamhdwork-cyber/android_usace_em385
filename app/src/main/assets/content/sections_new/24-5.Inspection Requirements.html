24-5 Inspection Requirements.

   a.  Accessways.  Inspect means of access daily to ensure they provide safe access and are free of hazards, obstructions, and do not impede or restrict travel of personnel.  (24-5.a)

   b.  Ladders.  Inspect ladders according to the manufacturer’s instructions.  Inspect for visible defects prior to placing into service, before each use, and after any occurrence that could affect their safe use.  Immediately tag broken or damaged ladders with “DO NOT USE”, or with similar wording, and withdraw them from service until restored to a condition meeting their original design.  (24-5.b)

   c.  Personnel Hoists and Elevators.  Inspect personnel hoists and elevators according to the manufacturer’s instructions and the applicable ANSI, ASME, and OSHA Standards (that is, ASME A17, ANSI A10.4, 29 CFR 1926.552, ANSI A10.22).  (24-5.c)
