12-6 Activity Hazard Analysis (AHA) Requirements.

Develop AHAs according to paragraphs 1-6 or 2-6, as applicable.  Additionally, AHAs involving HEC must adhere to the following:

   a.  When the activity requires the control of hazardous energy, a HIP for the activity being performed must be attached to and referenced in the AHA.  An AHA by itself will not be considered a HIP.  For AHAs that have been previously accepted by the KO or COR, the HIP alone must be submitted for review.  (12-6.a)

   b.  When preparing the AHA, the HIP must also be reviewed by an Authorized Individual to ensure it is still valid and that there have been no changes in the configuration that would require a change in the HIP.  (12-6.b)

   c.  The AHA and the HIP must be provided to and accepted by the KO or COR prior to performing these activities.  (12-6.c)
