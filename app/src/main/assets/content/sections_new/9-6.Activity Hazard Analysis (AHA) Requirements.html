9-6 Activity Hazard Analysis (AHA) Requirements.

Develop AHAs according to paragraphs 1-6 or 2-6, as applicable.  

   a.  AHAs are required when conducting wildland fire prescribed burns (see paras 9-7.b and 9-8.l).  (9-6.a)

   b.  Employer will determine how long a fire watch is required to stay in place, but it must be a minimum of thirty (30) minutes after the exposure has ended and documented in the AHA (see para 9-8.g).  (9-6.b)
