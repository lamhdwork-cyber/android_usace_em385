23-5 Inspection Requirements.

   a.  Inspect all equipment prior to each use and maintain and use it according to the manufacturer’s instructions.  (23-5.a)

   b.  A Rope Access Supervisor/Rope Qualified Person (QP) for Rope Access or a Rope Access Lead Technician/CP for Rope Access must inspect equipment at least annually according to the manufacturer’s instructions.  Document the inspections.  (23-5.b)
