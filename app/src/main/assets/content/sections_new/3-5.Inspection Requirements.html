3-5. Inspection Requirements.

   a.  First Aid Kits.  Check the contents of first aid kits prior to their use onsite and at least every 3 months when work is in progress.  Ensure that they are complete, in good condition, and have not expired.  (3-5.a)

   b.  Automatic External Defibrillator (AED).  Document battery and functionality checks at least monthly or according to the manufacturer’s instructions, or applicable federal, state, or local requirements, whichever is more stringent.  (3-5.b)
