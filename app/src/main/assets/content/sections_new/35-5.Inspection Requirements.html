35-5 Inspection Requirements.

   a.  Inspect all USACE-operated food service facilities and facilities operated under USACE contracts, including galleys aboard vessels, according to TB MED 530 and the USPHS Food Code.  (35-5.a)

   b.  High Risk Facilities.  Periodic sanitation inspections of food preparation areas of high risk facilities must be conducted and documented according to TB MED 530 table 8-2, at least quarterly.  (35-5.b)
