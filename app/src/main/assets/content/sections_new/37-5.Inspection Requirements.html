37-5 Inspection Requirements.

   a.  All applicable inspections identified in other chapters of this manual are still applicable for emergency operations mission assignments.  This includes, but is not limited to, flood-fighting and Federal Emergency Management Agency missions assigned to USACE.  (37-5.a)

   b.  All incinerator placements must be inspected by a USACE SOH professional prior to the start of burning (see para 37-8.i).  (37-5.b)
