Safety and Occupational Health Program Management for Contractors

This chapter applies to all contractors performing work activities under a USACE contract including those administered on behalf of USACE. This chapter provides the overall programmatic guidance for developing, managing, and implementing a SOH program. The requirements set forth in this chapter are the foundation for the contractor SOH program and must be carried through the additional chapters in this manual.

Note. The term Government Designated Authority (GDA) is no longer applicable within this manual. GDA has been replaced with the responsibility of the KO or COR.
