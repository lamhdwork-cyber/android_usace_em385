25-6 Activity Hazard Analysis (AHA) Requirements.

Develop AHAs according to paragraphs 1-6 or 2-6, as applicable.  An AHA is required to be developed by a CP for all work in excavations and trenches.  The AHA must include the following:

   a.  For all piping activities, include workers’ increased exposure during connection activities (for example, bent over, kneeling).  (25-6.a)

   b.  Methods and locations for egress.  (25-6.b)

   c.  Identification and credentials of the CP, Excavation.  (25-6.c)

   d.  Documentation that examination of the ground by the CP results in no indication of a potential cave-in.  (25-6.d)
