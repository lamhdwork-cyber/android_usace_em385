10-4 Roles and Responsibilities.

   a.  Critical Weld Inspectors.  CWSI, CWI, or equivalent inspector must inspect all welds on critical items.  (10-4.a)

   b.  Competent Person (CP).  A CP must evaluate hazards to determine what controls are needed for welding, cutting, and heating operations that do not involve conditions or materials described in this chapter.  (10-4.b)
